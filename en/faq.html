Jeju Duckgu Samseong: frequently asked questions

Location and hours

Where is Jeju Duckgu Samseong and how do I get there?

Jeju Duckgu Samseong is at 1F, 23-9 Samseong-ro 100-gil, Gangnam-gu, Seoul (서울 강남구 삼성로100길 23-9 1층). It is about a 7-minute walk from Samseong Jungang Station (Line 9) Exit 5 and about 10-11 minutes from Samseong Station (Line 2) Exit 5. Look for the blue storefront, and show the Korean address to a taxi driver.

Is it close to COEX and Seven Luck Casino?

Yes. Jeju Duckgu Samseong is about 170 m in a straight line from Seven Luck Casino Gangnam COEX (based on Naver Map locations). Many guests come after trade shows, conferences and business meetings at COEX. Evening tables fill quickly during COEX events, so please book by phone.

What are the opening hours?

Jeju Duckgu Samseong is open Monday to Friday 11:00-22:00 with a break from 14:30 to 16:00, and Saturday 16:00-22:00. It is closed on Sundays.

Do you serve lunch?

Yes, Jeju Duckgu Samseong serves lunch Monday to Friday, 11:00-14:30. Kimchi stew and soybean-paste stew set meals cost KRW 10,900, and Jeju-style spicy stir-fried pork costs KRW 11,900 per person (minimum two people). There is no lunch service on Saturdays.

Ordering

Do you have an English menu?

Yes. Jeju Duckgu Samseong has menus in English, Japanese and Chinese, and staff grill the meat for you, so you do not need to know how Korean BBQ works.

Do the staff grill the meat for us?

Yes. At Jeju Duckgu Samseong, staff sear the meat, cut it into bite-size pieces and grill it at your table until it is ready. Kimchi, bean sprouts and bracken are grilled alongside.

What should we order?

At Jeju Duckgu Samseong, the Duckgu Hangeun set suits two people (pork belly 400 g and neck 200 g, KRW 63,000). For three or four, the Omok set (KRW 84,000) adds more neck, anchovy stew and rice. For four or five, the Monttak set (KRW 164,000) adds prime boneless short rib and pork jowl. Pork belly and neck are also on the menu as 200 g portions at KRW 19,000 each.

How do I eat Korean BBQ here?

At Jeju Duckgu Samseong you do not need to cook anything: staff grill the meat and cut it for you. Dip the pork in anchovy sauce (meljeot) or the other sauces on the table, and eat it with the grilled kimchi, bean sprouts and bracken. Bracken and side dishes are refillable at the self-service bar.

How do I know the prices are fair for tourists?

The prices on this website come from Jeju Duckgu Samseong's own Korean menu on Naver Place (as of 3 October 2026), and the restaurant has menus in English, Japanese and Chinese. On Naver, the 73 visitors who left a star rating gave an average of 4.95 out of 5 (as of 12 October 2026).

How much does a meal cost?

At Jeju Duckgu Samseong, one portion of Jeju pork (200 g of belly or neck, or 160 g of jowl or gabeurisal) costs KRW 19,000. Sets cost KRW 63,000-192,000 and weekday lunch sets start at KRW 10,900. Prices include tax and there is no tipping in Korea.

Is it Jeju black pork?

Jeju Duckgu Samseong serves Jeju pork delivered from Jeju Island three times a week.

What is meljeot or meljorim?

Mel is the Jeju word for anchovy. Meljeot is salted, fermented anchovy sauce, and meljorim is Jeju Duckgu Samseong's anchovy stew (KRW 8,000), simmered for about 7 minutes until the whole anchovies are soft. Many guests dip grilled pork in it. Other dipping sauces are also served if you do not like anchovy.

Is there anything for people who do not eat pork?

Jeju Duckgu Samseong specialises in pork, but the menu also has prime boneless short rib (KRW 20,000, 20 portions a day) and butter-grilled squid (KRW 12,000).

What drinks do you have?

Jeju Duckgu Samseong serves Jeju-themed drinks, including a Jeju hallabong citrus highball (KRW 7,000). You may also bring your own wine (corkage KRW 20,000 per bottle) or spirits (KRW 30,000 per bottle).

Booking and payment

How do I book a table?

Call Jeju Duckgu Samseong on +82-507-1460-8793, or message us on Naver TalkTalk if you have a Naver account.

Can I walk in without a booking?

Usually yes. In 230 Naver visitor reviews written between November 2024 and October 2026, 210 said they were seated without waiting. Some reviews mention a full house, so booking is recommended for weekday evenings and large groups.

Groups and families

Can you take a large group?

Yes. Jeju Duckgu Samseong has a 16-seat area and a 20-seat area and accepts groups of 10 to 40. The whole restaurant can be booked for about 36-40 people with a minimum spend of KRW 1,500,000. There are no private rooms.

Can we come with children?

Yes. Families with children often visit Jeju Duckgu Samseong, and staff do the grilling, so you can keep an eye on the children. Hallabong citrus juice is available for kids.

Will my clothes smell of barbecue?

At Jeju Duckgu Samseong, coats and bags go into storage under the seat, which has a fragrance sheet inside. Hair ties are also provided.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes. Jeju Duckgu Samseong has free Wi-Fi, separate men's and women's restrooms, and phone chargers at the tables.
