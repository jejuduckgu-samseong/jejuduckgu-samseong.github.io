Jeju Duckgu Samseong (제주덕구 삼성점) is a Korean BBQ restaurant near COEX and Samseong Station in Gangnam, Seoul, where staff grill Jeju pork, delivered from Jeju Island three times a week, at your table.

Jeju Duckgu Samseong is a Korean BBQ restaurant near Samseong Station and COEX. We serve Jeju pork belly and neck, delivered from Jeju three times a week, in 200 g portions, plus special cuts such as gabeurisal and pork jowl, and prime boneless short rib limited to 20 portions a day.

Popular sides are the brisket soybean-paste rice stew, butter-grilled squid and cold kimchi noodles. Many guests dip their pork in our Jeju-style anchovy stew.

Weekday lunch

Jeju Duckgu Samseong serves lunch Monday to Friday, 11:00-14:30. Kimchi stew or beef-brisket soybean-paste stew set meals cost KRW 10,900, and Jeju-style spicy stir-fried pork costs KRW 11,900 per person (minimum two people). There is no lunch service on Saturdays.

FAQ

What are the opening hours?

Jeju Duckgu Samseong is open Monday to Friday 11:00-22:00 with a break from 14:30 to 16:00, and Saturday 16:00-22:00. It is closed on Sundays.

Do you have an English menu?

Yes. Jeju Duckgu Samseong has menus in English, Japanese and Chinese, and staff grill the meat for you, so you do not need to know how Korean BBQ works.

Do the staff grill the meat for us?

Yes. At Jeju Duckgu Samseong, staff sear the meat, cut it into bite-size pieces and grill it at your table until it is ready. Kimchi, bean sprouts and bracken are grilled alongside.

How much does a meal cost?

At Jeju Duckgu Samseong, one portion of Jeju pork (200 g of belly or neck, or 160 g of jowl or gabeurisal) costs KRW 19,000. Sets cost KRW 63,000-192,000 and weekday lunch sets start at KRW 10,900. Prices include tax and there is no tipping in Korea.

How do I book a table?
